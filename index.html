What the conference covers

MATEX 2027 is hosted by the Department of Metallurgy & Materials Engineering at COEP Technological University together with the Indian Institute of Metals, Pune Chapter. Its focus is sustainable, intelligent and high-performance materials.

The programme is organised around four engineering themes: green metallurgy and circular materials, AI and materials informatics, materials for extreme environments, and energy materials for electric mobility. Sessions are built for technical depth and for academia–industry collaboration.

EST. 1948 · ORGANISING DEPARTMENT

Department of Metallurgy & Materials Engineering, COEP Tech

Founded in 1948 as the Department of Metallurgical Engineering, MME is a long-standing centre for metallurgical and materials education in western Maharashtra. It is known for teaching, research and industrial consultancy, and its alumni include research scientists and leaders of major organisations.

Research is funded by DST, AICTE and industry, with collaborators including Cummins India, ARAI, Ador Welding, JSW Steel, DIAT and C-MET. Graduates join companies such as Tata Steel, JSW Steel, ArcelorMittal, Tata Motors and Bharat Forge.

EST. 1854 · PUNE

COEP Technological University

One of the oldest engineering institutions in Asia, COEP has trained engineers in Pune for more than 170 years. Its Department of Metallurgy & Materials Engineering works across physical and mechanical metallurgy, processing, characterisation and advanced materials.

EST. 1946 · PUNE CHAPTER

Indian Institute of Metals

India's professional society for metallurgists and materials engineers, with chapters across the country. The Pune Chapter links the city's academic institutions with its forging, casting, automotive and materials-processing industry.

Scientific programme

Four grand engineering themes

Submissions are invited in any of the areas below. Topic lists are indicative; related work is welcome.

01GM

Green Metallurgy, Critical Minerals & Circular Materials

  • Sustainable extraction & processing
  • Low-carbon metallurgy (H₂-DRI)
  • Resource efficiency & circular economy
  • Recycling, urban mining & e-waste
  • Critical minerals for strategic sectors
  • Sustainable processing & climate technologies
02AI

AI, Materials Informatics & ICME

  • Machine learning for materials discovery
  • Data-driven alloy and process design
  • Computational materials & CALPHAD
  • Integrated Computational Materials Engineering
  • Digital twins & process modelling
  • Autonomous materials laboratories
  • Process and property prediction
03AX

Advanced Materials & Manufacturing for Extreme Environments

  • High-performance alloys, including advanced steels and high-entropy alloys
  • Aerospace, space & defence materials
  • High-temperature & radiation-resistant materials
  • Additive manufacturing & advanced processing
  • Harsh service: corrosion, creep & failure analysis
04EV

Energy Materials & Electric Mobility

  • Battery materials (Li-ion, solid-state, Na-ion)
  • Hydrogen production, storage & fuel cells
  • Materials for electric vehicles
  • Renewable energy materials (solar, thermal, wind)
  • Energy storage & grid materials
  • Recycling & second-life materials
  • Safety, degradation & lifetime prediction

Present your work at MATEX 2027

Original, unpublished research is invited for oral and poster presentation in any of the four themes.

COEP Technological University, Pune

Wellesley Road, Shivajinagar, Pune, Maharashtra 411005, India. The campus sits on the bank of the Mula river, a short drive from the city centre.

Getting there

By air

Pune International Airport (PNQ), Lohegaon, has domestic and selected international connections. Mumbai (BOM) is about 3–4 hours by road.

By rail

Pune Junction and Shivajinagar railway stations are both close to campus, with direct trains from Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

By metro & road

Pune Metro's Shivajinagar station is near the campus. Auto-rickshaws and app cabs are widely available.
